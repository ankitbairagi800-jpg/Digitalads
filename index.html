❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need good internet or a headset to use SpeakGuru? +
No special headset needed! SpeakGuru works with any built-in microphone on your phone or laptop. A stable internet connection (even 4G) is sufficient for smooth practice sessions.
Is SpeakGuru only for people who know no English? +
Not at all! SpeakGuru is designed for all levels — from absolute beginners to upper-intermediate learners who want to sound more confident and professional. Our AI adapts to wherever you are.
Will the AI understand my Indian accent? +
Yes! This is our biggest differentiator. SpeakGuru's AI is specifically trained on Indian English patterns from 12+ regional language backgrounds. It understands your accent AND helps you improve it — without trying to make you sound "American" or "British".
How is this different from Duolingo or British Council? +
SpeakGuru focuses exclusively on spoken English with real-time AI voice feedback — not text exercises or vocabulary quizzes. It's built for Indian learners, uses Indian contexts (offices, markets, interviews), and gives you practical speaking practice from Day 1.
Can I really see results in 30 days? +
Yes, with just 15 minutes of practice per day! Our data shows that 87% of users report a noticeable improvement in their spoken English confidence within 30 days. The key is consistency — even 3-4 days a week makes a big difference.
Is my voice data safe? +
Absolutely. Your voice recordings are processed in real-time for feedback and are not stored on our servers. We comply with India's IT Act data protection requirements and GDPR standards.
